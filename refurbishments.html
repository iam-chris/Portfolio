Refurbishments

Giving old hardware a second life — diagnosing faults, upgrading components, and getting machines ready for their next owner.

My Process

Every refurbishment follows the same four steps to make sure the machine leaves in better shape than it arrived.

01

Diagnose

Run hardware diagnostics to find failing drives, bad RAM, or thermal issues before touching anything.

02

Repair & Upgrade

Replace failing parts, swap in an SSD, add RAM, and repaste the CPU where it'll make a real difference.

03

Clean & Test

Deep clean the case and internals, reinstall the OS, and stress-test to confirm everything's stable.

04

Resell or Repurpose

List it for a fair price or repurpose it into a homelab box, depending on what the machine's best suited for.
