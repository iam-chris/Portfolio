About Me

The story behind the projects — how I got into IT, and what keeps me tinkering.

Hi, I'm Chris — welcome to my corner of the internet.

I got my start in tech by taking apart an old family desktop just to see what was inside it — and I haven't really stopped since. These days I split my time between networking projects, homelab experiments, and refurbishing computers that would otherwise end up in a landfill.

I like understanding how things work end to end: why a packet takes the route it does, why a machine won't POST, or why a service keeps crashing at 2am. Most of what's on this site started as a personal problem I wanted to solve, or a piece of hardware I wanted to save.

When I'm not working on the homelab, I'm usually reading release notes, browsing local listings for cheap hardware, or helping a neighbor get their Wi-Fi working again.

My Journey

A quick timeline of how I ended up here.

2018

First build

Built my first gaming PC and got hooked on troubleshooting hardware issues.

2020

Discovered Linux

Installed Ubuntu on an old laptop and started learning the command line out of curiosity.

2022

Started the homelab

Picked up a used mini PC and started experimenting with self-hosting and virtualization.

2024

Began refurbishing

Started buying broken PCs, fixing them up, and reselling or donating them locally.

Today

Still tinkering

Continuing to build out the homelab, take on new networking projects, and document it all here.
